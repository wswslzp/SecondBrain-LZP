# Daniela Amodei

[[anthropic]] 联合创始人兼 **President**，兄妹搭档中的**妹妹、operator**。角色是把 [[dario-amodei|Dario]]「swirling cosmic thoughts」落地为可执行的公司运作。

> "Daniela, the sister and operator who puts Dario's swirling cosmic thoughts into action."

## 背景

- 与 Dario 是互补型（complements）：Dario 从小痴迷数学与科幻，Daniela「more into reading and arts」。
- 职业起点是 **Stripe 早期员工**。
- 曾与 Dario 及丈夫 **Holden Karnofsky** 同住旧金山；2016 年 Dario 加入 OpenAI 后她随后加入，2021 年共同创立 Anthropic。
- 兄妹关系：「we've always been really close since we were little, but I think we always really wanted to do something big together.」被问吵架谁赢，答「No one. No one.」

## 与 [[generalist]] 阅读清单的连接

Daniela 的丈夫 **Holden Karnofsky** 著有《Learning by Writing》，恰好收录在 [[generalist]] 的通才阅读清单中——一个跨页面的巧合交叉引用。

## 安全叙事

作为 operator，她也是公司安全定位的对外声音之一：

> "All of this technical safety research that we do, it's to make the products more aligned with human values, to make sure that we're not building something that could cause societal issues that previous technology companies have accidentally caused."
