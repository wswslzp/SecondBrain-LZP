# Dario Amodei

[[anthropic]] 联合创始人兼 CEO，兄妹搭档中的**兄长、visionary**。以警告 AI 风险闻名的「unlikely AI celebrity」，如今掌舵一家估值近万亿美元的 AI frontrunner。

> "Dario, the brother and visionary, and Daniela, the sister and operator who puts Dario's swirling cosmic thoughts into action."

## 背景与轨迹

- 童年在旧金山，父亲是皮匠（leather craftsman），母亲在图书馆工作。
- 对第一次互联网革命「毫无兴趣」，只痴迷数学、理解宇宙、科幻。中学修微积分，高中在 UC Berkeley 上数学课。
- **神经科学**背景 → 转向 AI，先后在 **Baidu**、**Google**。
- 2016 年加入新成立的 **OpenAI**（Daniela 随后加入），当时曾与 Daniela 及其丈夫 Holden Karnofsky 同住旧金山。
- 因**信任与价值观**（不仅是 safety 分歧）离开 OpenAI，2021 年共同创立 Anthropic。

### 离开 OpenAI

> "There are many valid disagreements to be had on safety... but that alone is not sufficient to leave. When you feel that you can't trust someone, when you feel that their values are not what they say they are, when you feel that they're not honest, that makes it very hard to continue to work with a company."

「why argue with someone when you don't have the same vision and you don't trust them? The way to resolve it is you go off and do your thing.」

## 提出 scaling laws

在 OpenAI 期间提出 [[scaling-laws]]：即使底层算法不变，LLM 也会随 data + compute 增加而可预测地变强。当时是「反主流」的科学观点，为 GPT/ChatGPT 铺路。

> "That was sort of an unusual counter-cultural scientific perspective that I think was really held by our founding research team."

## 对就业冲击的直言

- AI 可能在 **1–5 年**消灭 **50%** 的 entry-level 白领岗位（一年前预测，如今仍是「同一量级的担忧」）。
- 核心区分：**tasks vs jobs**（见 [[tasks-vs-jobs]]）——自动化 90% 的工作让人在剩下 10% 上 10× leveraged，但最终逼近 100% 时必须给人找别的事做（the hump）。
- 担忧「高 GDP 增长 + 高失业/underemployment + 高不平等」的不寻常组合。
- 情绪坦诚：「It's very uncomfortable.」但认为这正是他与许多人选择 Anthropic 的原因——「here we can hopefully make it go a little bit better」。

### 反驳「doom marketing」指控

Jensen Huang 称他「conflating tasks with jobs」、AI 在创造岗位；有人说这是有利于 Anthropic 的 doom marketing。Dario 强硬回击：

> "In every interview I talk about the possible ways to address these risks, from tax and macroeconomic policy to what the new jobs are... I have like five pages where I lay out the difference between tasks and jobs... the idea that this is cheap marketing is itself cheap marketing."

他把断章取义归咎于 Silicon Valley 的「三秒短视频」社媒病（social media, which I detest）。

## 安全哲学与自我定位

- 长文《Machines of Loving Grace》《The Adolescence of Technology》。
- 最爱《The Making of the Atomic Bomb》；自比 **Leo Szilard**（最先想到链式反应者）而非 Oppenheimer——视 Oppenheimer 为「failure case，what should not happen」。
- 「we're not gonna get through this with larger-than-life personalities... the only way it's gonna end well for everyone is if there is basically **checks and balances everywhere**.」
