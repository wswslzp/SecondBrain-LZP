## Core Thesis

Trillions of dollars of value will be created over the next 5 years by a new kind of company. Over the next 20 years, it will be **the only kind of company and the only job any human can have**: Context Farming.

## Key Quote from an Unnamed Founder

> "You aren't smarter than AI anymore. What that means is your job is no longer to do the work or even make decisions. Your job is to give the AI as much context as possible so it can make the decisions and do the work."

- Shared at a dinner of founders already building this way
- ~Half the table had independently reached the same conclusion and restructured their companies around it
- All had built a **"company brain"** — a single place aggregating all context so agents can run the business
- Results: **zero to tens of millions in revenue, in months, with tiny teams**

## The Agentic Micro Company (AMC)

Goldstein references Jack Dorsey and Sequoia's Roelof Botha's essay *From Hierarchy to Intelligence*, arguing the 2,000-year-old Roman-inspired corporate hierarchy (massive specialized teams, layers of management) was designed to disseminate information constrained by the human mind — and is now obsolete.

**Dorsey announced a 40% layoff** and restructured Block into tiny teams using agents — the same model.

### AMC Structure:
- **Tiny teams** of generalist human "player-coaches"
- **Tons of agents** executing the majority of work
- **Custom software** to automate and monitor the business
- **Company Brain** serving as both context for agents and backend for software

AMCs are optimized for speed and make **everyone at a company a founder** (echoing Brian Chesky's "Founder Mode").

## The Company Brain

**"Context is king, but for the most part, the emperor has had no clothes."**

### Key insights:
- Even **small models outperform larger models** when they have better context
- More emails, meeting notes, and documents fed to an agent → more powerful it becomes
- Goldstein's **Micro agent outperforms Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, & Hermes agents** for most queries — simply because it has an advanced memory storage/retrieval system

> "Today's agents are basically quantum physics PhDs with severe amnesia."

### Industry awakening:
- **Karpathy's viral post** (April 2, 2026) on knowledge graphs catalyzed industry awareness of unified memory systems
- Open-source ecosystem exploded (e.g., **Garry Tan's GBrain**)
- Best approaches approximate **human brain functions**: long-term memory, episodic memory, short-term memory, etc.

## The Context Farmer

**As model intelligence expands, the role of the human contracts.**

The farming metaphor:
- Farmers don't produce fruit — **plants do**
- Farmers create conditions: crop rotation, water, sunlight
- Context Farmers **monitor context quality and decay**, add context from the physical world only humans can access, and feed the Company Brain

> "It's what great executives already do with high-performing teams. As the saying goes: hire great people and get out of their way."

People doing this well have **ample free time** — they take open-ended meetings, collect new ideas, and feed them back. **They're not busy. They're farming.**

## The Trillion-Dollar Terraform Opportunity

### The Problem Today
Building context infrastructure is like farming barren desert — people must set up databases, ingestion pipelines, and systems from scratch. Open-source tools exist but fall short.

### The Vision: Composable Global Context
Existing platforms organized information but are **siloed and non-composable**:
- **Google** → world's websites
- **Meta** → world's people
- **LinkedIn** → world's professionals
- **Salesforce** → world's deals

### Why Composability Matters
**Composability** (combining independent parts into new systems) has been the catalyst for virtually every technological revolution in history.

> "The Intelligence Revolution will be catalyzed not by intelligence itself, but by **composable context**. A global brain powering millions of AMCs and trillions of agents."

The trillion-dollar opportunity: **terraforming the context layer** — evolving beyond MD files and siloed company brains to a **singular global brain** that serves the entire ecosystem, making any information, software, or business outcome **a prompt away**.
