**Game theory defines luck as** "Hidden Information"博弈论将运气定义为“Hidden Information”

Game theory suggests that what we perceive as luck is frequently just information we do not have yet.博弈论表明，我们所认为的运气往往只是我们尚未掌握的信息。

- **Simulated Uncertainty:** Games with dice or card draws model scenarios where crucial information is hidden.模拟不确定性：掷骰子或抽牌的游戏模拟了关键信息被隐藏的情景。
- **The Goal:** The goal is to move from a state of total ignorance (luck-driven) to a state of calculated risk, where you understand the probability distribution of potential outcomes.目标：目标是从完全无知的状态（由运气驱动）转向计算风险的状态，在那里你理解潜在结果的概率分布。

It also teaches us about **Optimal Strategy:** Even with random elements, optimal strategies can be developed to maximize the probability of success over many rounds, even if a single round is lost to bad luck.它还教会我们最优策略：即使有随机元素，也可以开发出最优策略，在多轮中最大化成功的概率，即使单轮因运气不好而失败。

> **The "Luck Equation" (Preparation & Opportunity):** A formula for capturing luck is (Quality x Volume/serendipity + openness). “运气方程”（准备与机会）：捕捉运气的公式是（质量 × 数量/机缘巧合 + 开放性）。

The people you think are lucky are just more prepared than you are. They have prepared more for when opportunities arise. They have maximized their surface area for luck to find them. 你认为幸运的人，其实只是比你准备得更充分。 他们为机会来临时做了更多准备。 他们最大化了运气找到他们的“接触面”。

"Opportunity cost is **the value of the next-best alternative that is given up when making a decision between two or more options**. It represents the benefits or value - which can be financial, time, or effort - that is foregone when choosing one option over another, highlighting the concept of scarcity and the need for trade-offs in decision-making."机会成本是在两个或多个选项之间做出决策时，所放弃的次优备选方案的价值。它代表了选择一个选项而非另一个选项时所放弃的好处或价值——这些价值可以是金钱、时间或精力——从而突显了稀缺性的概念以及决策中权衡取舍的必要性。

YOU can cultivate luck. YOU can become luckier. YOU can increase your luck by **creating opportunities for chance through small disruptions in routine**.你能培养运气。 你能变得更幸运。 你可以通过在日常习惯中进行小幅扰动来创造偶然机会，从而增加你的运气。

> The 4 key principles of luck - Richard Wiseman运气的 4 个关键原则 - Richard Wiseman

1. Being skilled at creating and noticing chance opportunities (this can include nurturing your network of friends and colleagues who can expose you to more opportunities)擅长创造和发现偶然机会（这包括培养你的朋友和同事网络，让他们为你带来更多机会）
2. Making lucky decisions by listening to your intuition通过倾听直觉做出幸运的决定
3. Creating self-fulfilling prophecies via positive expectations通过积极预期创造自我实现的预言
4. Adopting a resilient attitude that transforms bad luck into good培养一种坚韧的态度，将坏运气转化为好运

> Two types of luck:两种运气：

**Blind luck.** Opportunity, or outcome, without effort. Unforeseen, and more important, uncontrollable. 盲运气。没有努力的机会或结果。不可预见，更重要的是，不可控制。

**Luck from motion.** Taking action. Trying things. Doing things. Trying and being prepared for when opportunity arises. 行动带来的运气。采取行动。尝试事情。做事情。尝试并为机会到来做好准备。

**YOU can cultivate both. 你能培养两者。**

## MAXIMIZE YOUR SURFACE AREA 最大化你的表面积

**"Maximizing your "luck surface area" means actively increasing the number of opportunities for fortunate events to occur by blending passionate action with public visibility"最大化你的“luck surface area”意味着通过将热情行动与公开可见性相结合，积极增加幸运事件发生的机会数量**

> **Do Passionate Work (Do): 做热爱的工作 (Do):**

- **Increase Volume:** Create more, try more, and initiate more projects.增加数量：创造更多，尝试更多，并启动更多项目。
- **Focus on Passion:** Working on things you are passionate about makes you better at them, creating higher-quality "bait" for luck.专注于热情：从事你热情的事物会让你做得更好，从而创造出更高品质的“bait”来吸引运气。
- **Take Risks:** Be comfortable with failure and step into work before it becomes your full-time job. 敢于冒险：习惯失败，并在它成为你的全职工作之前就开始投入其中。

> **Share Your Work (Tell): 分享你的作品（Tell）：**

- **Publish Publicly:** Share your work, journey, and learnings on social media, blogs, or newsletters.公开发布：将你的作品、历程和学习心得分享到社交媒体、博客或新闻通讯上。
- **Network with Generosity:** Connect with people to build relationships, not just contacts, and offer help without expecting immediate returns.慷慨拓展人脉：与人建立关系，而非仅仅收集联系人，并提供帮助而不期待即时回报。
- **Be Visible:** Ensure your efforts are searchable and "crawlable" by others to improve serendipitous discovery. 保持可见：确保你的努力可被他人搜索和“爬取”，以提升偶然发现的机会。

> **Create Exposure: 创建曝光：**

- **Say "Yes" More:** Accept invitations and opportunities that arise to expand your network.多说“是”：接受出现的邀请和机会，以扩展你的社交网络。
- **Change Environments:** Move to new places or join new communities to change the flow of luck in your life.改变环境：搬到新地方或加入新社区，以改变你生活中的运气流动。
- **Be Consistent:** Luck is a function of both the surface area and the time of exposure; keep going保持一致：运气取决于暴露的表面积和暴露时间；继续坚持

Game theory teaches us that luck is not merely random chance, but a factor to be managed, mitigated, and sometimes manipulated through strategic decision-making. While luck represents outcomes outside a player's control, game theory provides the tools to minimize the impact of bad luck and maximize the advantage of good luck over the long term.博弈论教导我们，运气并非仅仅是随机事件，而是一个可以通过战略决策来管理、缓解、有时甚至操控的因素。运气代表了玩家无法控制的结果，而博弈论则提供了工具，帮助我们在长期内最小化坏运的影响，并最大化好运的优势。

> Strategy is Controlling What You Can策略就是掌控你所能掌控的

Game theory separates the controllable from the uncontrollable.博弈论将可控因素与不可控因素区分开来。

- **Luck vs. Choice:** Too much luck can overwhelm choice. The goal of strategy is to create scenarios where your choices matter more than random chance.运气 vs. 选择：过多的运气会压倒选择。策略的目标是创造这样的情境，让你的选择比随机运气更重要。
- **Pre-Action vs. Post-Action Luck:** "Pre-action" luck allows you to make decisions based on the new information, making it easier to mitigate than "post-action" luck (like rolling to see if an attack succeeded).Pre-Action vs. Post-Action Luck：“行动前”运气允许你基于新信息做出决策，比“行动后”运气更容易缓解（例如掷骰查看攻击是否成功）。
- **Mitigation:** By studying probability, players can make decisions that shift the odds in their favor, turning a "game of luck" into a "game of skill". 缓解：通过学习概率，玩家可以做出将胜算转向有利于自己的决定，将“运气游戏”转变为“技巧游戏”。

**Ultimately, game theory teaches us to behave like a skilled poker player: Accept the cards you are dealt, but make the best possible decisions based on them.最终，博弈论教导我们像一位熟练的扑克玩家一样行事：接受你被发到的牌，但基于它们做出最佳可能的决策。**
