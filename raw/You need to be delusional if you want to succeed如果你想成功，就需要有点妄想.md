Tell me if any of these sound like you:告诉我以下这些是否听起来像你：

**1) You feel like you’ve been lied to about what a good life looks like.1）你觉得自己在“美好生活应该是什么样”这件事上被骗了。**

You’re pursuing a goal that sounds nice when you mention it at dinner with grandma. Or a goal that wouldn’t make your parents think you’re a disappointment. And you don’t see it going anywhere.你在追求一个在奶奶家吃饭时提起来显得很体面的目标。或者是一个不会让父母觉得你令人失望的目标。而你看不到它有任何进展。

If you ignore that feeling, you wake up 10 years later with a degree, decent job, and a never ending sense of despair that you’ll never be able to chase your own dreams.如果你忽视这种感觉，十年后你醒来时，会拥有学位、体面的工作，以及一种永无止境的绝望感——觉得自己永远无法追逐自己的梦想。

**2) You’re productive, but not actually making progress toward anything.2）你很高效，但实际上没有朝着任何目标取得真正的进展。**

You wake up early. You listen to podcasts. You journal, you lift, your calendar is full, but you don’t know where you’re going.你早起。你听播客。你写日记，你健身，你的日程表排得满满当当，但你不知道自己要去向何方。

Give it 5 years and you’ll still be “figuring out what to do with your life.”再过 5 年，你仍然在“琢磨这辈子要做什么”。

**3) You’re ambitious, but stay quiet because it sounds embarrassing to your friends and family.3）你很有抱负，但保持沉默，因为这在朋友和家人听来很尴尬。**

You want a unique life. You don’t want to fit in. But when people ask about your future, you water it down so nobody laughs at it.你想要独特的人生。你不想随大流。但当别人问起你的未来时，你却把它说得轻描淡写，以免被人嘲笑。

You get stuck performing mental masturbation. You feel special for wanting to be unique, but you still do the same thing as everyone else.你陷入了精神自慰。你因为想要独特而觉得自己很特别，但你做的事仍然和其他人一模一样。

Nobody wants to end up in any of those 3 situations.没有人想最终落入这三种处境中的任何一种。

This letter will change that for you.这封信将为你改变这一点。

Because one of my favorite things to do is study the actions of both successful and mediocre people. And that’s all success really is. Doing more of what leads to success and less of what leads to mediocrity. The next few letters will be dedicated to just that.因为我最喜欢做的事情之一，就是研究成功者和平庸者的行为。而成功说到底不过如此：多做那些通向成功的事，少做那些通向平庸的事。接下来的几封信将专门探讨这一点。

When we reverse engineer the people who have done the impossible - Elon Musk, Steve Jobs, and any of the other greatest creatives, thinkers, and builders of the past - there is one pattern I’ve noticed between them all.当我们逆向剖析那些完成不可能之事的人——埃隆·马斯克、史蒂夫·乔布斯，以及过去其他最伟大的创造者、思想家和建设者——我注意到他们之间有一个共同模式。

Their goals are delusional.他们的目标近乎妄想。

That’s the key to their success.这正是他们成功的关键。

And that’s exactly what we’re going to go over - why average goals lead to failure, the psychology of delusional goals, how to enter the same reality as ultra successful people, and how to achieve what most people think is impossible.而这正是我们要探讨的内容——为什么平庸的目标会导致失败、妄想性目标的心理学、如何进入与超级成功者相同的现实，以及如何实现大多数人认为不可能的事情。

## Why “realistic” goals lead to failure and mediocrity (you fall into this category by default)为什么“现实”的目标会导致失败和平庸（你默认就属于这一类）

> The most common error of a smart engineer is to optimize a thing that should not exist. – Elon Musk聪明的工程师最常见的错误，就是去优化一个本不该存在的东西。 ——埃隆·马斯克

You have goals, even if you didn’t set them.你是有目标的，即使你并没有主动设定它们。

That’s how the mind works. And if you become aware of this, you can quickly see why you aren’t able to stick to any of the goals you set.大脑就是这样运作的。一旦你意识到这一点，你很快就能明白，为什么你无法坚持任何自己设定的目标。

We’re not talking about New Years Resolutions or some other surface level goal you set and immediately quit because you felt bad about how you looked in the mirror and forgot about it the next day (because another unconscious goal (usually convenience and comfort, or the goal of not drawing attention to yourself) was more important to you than finally getting in shape).我们说的不是新年决心，也不是那种因为你照镜子时对自己的样子感到不满意而设定、第二天就忘掉的表面目标（因为另一个无意识的目标——通常是便利和舒适，或者不引起别人注意的目标——对你来说比最终把身材练好更重要）。

We’re talking about how every thought you have or action you take is aiming toward a goal. Like a heat-seeking missile. You are silently optimizing for a goal you never chose, or someone else chose for you.我们说的是，你的每一个想法、每一个行动都在瞄准某个目标。就像一枚热追踪导弹。你在默默地为一个你从未选择、或是别人替你选择的目标进行优化。

Take a second and think about that.花点时间想一想这件事。

Every single thing you think or do is toward a goal.你思考或做的每一件事，都在朝着某个目标前进。

That’s important. Extremely important. It means that if you don’t know what those goals are, you are not in control of your life.这很重要。极其重要。这意味着，如果你不知道那些目标是什么，你就无法掌控自己的人生。

Here’s how it goes:事情是这样的：

- You are born你出生了
- Your first goal is survival你的第一个目标是生存
- Your parents have been thinking about what goals they want you to pursue for quite some time now. They “want the best for you”你的父母已经花了不少时间思考他们希望你追求什么样的目标。他们“想要给你最好的”。
- Your parents teach you how to walk, how to communicate, and eventually what their version of success for you looks like, which is usually the goals that society passed down to them (go to school, get a good job, praise your god)你的父母教你走路，教你沟通，最终教你他们心目中你该有的成功模样，而这通常是社会灌输给他们的目标（上学、找份好工作、赞美你的神）
- You go to school, then college because that’s just “what’s next”你去上学，然后上大学，因为那只是“接下来该做的事”
- You get the job, you hit the milestones, you check the boxes你找到工作，达成一个个里程碑，把清单上的框一个个打勾

And then, after almost a quarter of your life, you start to wonder if this was even what you wanted in the first place.然后，在过了差不多四分之一的人生之后，你开始怀疑这一切最初是否真的是你想要的。

> Entropy illustrates that systems trend toward disorder. Applied to the mind, psychic entropy means that your mind, and therefore your life, drifts toward disorder and chaos. At some point, a system becomes unstable and a new, more ordered structure must emerge, like how a faucet spurting out water eventually turns into a whirlpool. In your life, you hit a point where the lower part of you wants to stay the same and the higher part wants something more. It feels like you’re being ripped in half. That is a signal to completely turn your life around.熵表明系统趋向无序。将其应用于心智，心理熵意味着你的心智，进而你的生活，会逐渐滑向无序和混乱。 在某个时刻，系统会变得不稳定，必须出现一种新的、更有序的结构，就像水龙头喷出的水最终会形成漩涡一样。 在生活中，你会到达一个节点：你较低的部分想保持不变，而较高的部分想要更多。那感觉就像你被撕成了两半。这是一个彻底扭转人生的信号。

The reason realistic goals lead to failure and mediocrity is because of how you choose them. You look at what you think is possible, then water it down a bit more so that people won’t laugh or think you’re an idiot. A goal only feels realistic if it fits the life your parents, teachers, or five closest friends already expected you to have.现实的目标之所以会导致失败和平庸，是因为你选择它们的方式。你看着自己认为可能实现的事，然后再把它调低一点，好让别人不会嘲笑你或觉得你是个傻瓜。一个目标只有在符合你的父母、老师或五个最亲密的朋友早就预期你会过的那种生活时，才会让你觉得现实。

In other words, your mind is in a cage.换句话说，你的思想被囚禁在牢笼里。

And if you keep choosing to live in that cage, you’ll never have the chance to notice a potentially life-changing opportunity, let alone act on it, and your life slowly gets worse until the pain is so great you either get crushed or decide to pursue a delusional goal anyway.如果你继续选择活在那个牢笼里，你就永远没有机会注意到一个可能改变人生的机遇，更别提采取行动了；你的生活也会慢慢恶化，直到痛苦大到要么被压垮，要么索性决定去追求一个妄想的目标。

## The psychology of delusional goals妄想目标的心理

> The man who conceives himself to be a “failure-type person” will find some way to fail, in spite of all his good intentions, or his willpower, even if the opportunity is literally dumped in his lap. The person who conceives himself to be a victim of injustice, one “who was meant to suffer,” will invariably find circumstances to verify his opinions. – Maxwell Maltz一个自认为是“失败型的人”的人，尽管心怀善意、意志坚定，也总会找到某种方式失败，即使机会真的落到他怀里。一个自认为是不公正的受害者、认为自己“注定要受苦”的人，总会找到各种境况来证实自己的看法。 ——麦克斯威尔·马尔茨

Humans aren’t unique because they have memory or consciousness or a more refined intelligence than animals.人类并非独特，因为他们拥有记忆、意识或比动物更精细的智力。

They’re unique because they constantly simulate the future to steer the present.他们之所以独特，是因为他们不断模拟未来，以引导当下。

You think more about the future than the past, and the future is what causes your present behavior. Not the reverse. Your actions do not create the future. You create the future first, then act toward it.你想未来比想过去更多，而未来才是导致你当下行为的原因。不是反过来。你的行动并不会创造未来。你先创造未来，然后朝着它行动。

It’s called selective attention.这被称为选择性注意。

Your brain either gets pulled by stimuli or directed by a goal.你的大脑要么被各种刺激牵着走，要么被一个目标所引导。

And when you focus on one thing, that means, by definition, you’re not focused on the 99.99% of other things you could be focusing on. Your attention is goal directed rather than tossed around like a rag doll looking for the next cheap dopamine hit.而当你专注于一件事时，从定义上说，你就不再关注其他 99.99%本可以关注的事情。你的注意力是由目标引导的，而不是像布娃娃一样被抛来抛去，寻找下一次廉价的多巴胺刺激。

Delusional goals force this to happen.妄想般的目标会迫使这一切发生。

Imagine two people with drastically different goals.想象两个目标截然不同的人。

Tommy has the same goal as almost everyone else - go to school, get an education, get a decent job so he can finally afford a nice apartment, and acquire enough material status symbols to attract a mate.汤米的目标和几乎所有人一样——上学、接受教育、找一份体面的工作，这样他终于能负担得起一套不错的公寓，并获得足够的物质地位象征来吸引伴侣。

Johnny has a goal that seems impossible - build a content business that makes $100k a month in 6 months.约翰尼有一个看似不可能的目标——在 6 个月内打造一个每月能赚 10 万美元的内容业务。

Will Tommy ever have the possibility of making more money than what a “decent job” would pay him? Will Tommy ever learn something outside of school that allows him to pursue a more lucrative opportunity? No, because that’s not what his attention is selecting for. He could come across life changing information and his mind will consider it “noise” just because it’s not necessary to achieve the goal he’s pursuing.汤米是否有可能赚到比一份“体面工作”所能支付的更多的钱？汤米是否有可能在学校之外学到一些东西，让他能够追求更有利可图的机会？不会，因为那并不是他的注意力所选择关注的东西。他可能会接触到足以改变人生的信息，但他的大脑会把它当作“噪音”，仅仅因为它对于实现他正在追求的目标并非必要。

Johnny, on the other hand, cannot think like the average person. To achieve that delusional goal, he realizes that most actions he’s taking are completely useless.另一方面，Johnny 无法像普通人那样思考。为了实现那个妄想般的目标，他意识到自己正在做的大多数行动都毫无用处。

A properly set delusional goal pushes your brain into the unknown, accelerating neuroplasticity. Consistency reinforces pathways in your brain, but novelty and challenge stimulate learning and change faster. Flipping the switch and going all in on a goal throws your brain into an environment where it has no choice but to rewire, fast, and the new behavior becomes your new baseline.一个设定得当的妄想型目标会把你的大脑推向未知领域，加速神经可塑性。持续性会强化你大脑中的神经通路，但新奇和挑战能更快地刺激学习与改变。按下开关、全身心投入一个目标，会把你的大脑抛入一个别无选择、只能快速重塑的环境，而新行为会成为你的新基线。

That’s when flow state and obsession start to create a blissful quality of life. 5 intrinsic drivers start to stack.那时，心流状态和痴迷开始创造出一种幸福的生活品质。5 个内在驱动力开始叠加。

- **Curiosity** – The desire to explore the unknown, learn how to change, and fill knowledge gaps. Results in good dopamine from novelty and norepinephrine, which heightens attention, preparing you to learn.好奇心——探索未知、学习如何改变以及填补知识空白的渴望。它会因新奇感产生有益的多巴胺和去甲肾上腺素，从而提升注意力，让你为学习做好准备。
- **Passion** – An intense enthusiasm is built for the path that allows you to change your life. Results in more good dopamine and norepinephrine.热情——对那条能让你改变人生的道路建立起强烈的热忱。这会带来更多有益的多巴胺和去甲肾上腺素。
- **Purpose** – The feeling that your actions contribute to something larger than yourself. Achieving goals results in more dopamine which reinforces behavior. Serotonin stems from significance and belonging. Oxytocin stems from connection.目标感——一种你的行动在为比自己更宏大的事物做出贡献的感觉。实现目标会带来更多多巴胺，从而强化行为。血清素源于重要感和归属感。催产素源于联结。
- **Autonomy** – The desire to direct your own life and work. To control your choices, actions, and environment. Results in yet again more dopamine and a reduction in cortisol (the stress from feeling put in a box), allowing for creative decision making.自主——渴望主导自己的生活和工作的欲望。掌控自己的选择、行动和环境。这同样会带来更多多巴胺，并降低皮质醇（那种被塞进框里、处处受限所带来的压力），从而让你能够做出创造性决策。
- **Mastery** – The process of learning and growing is its own reward. Results in sustainable good dopamine that keeps you in the game.精通——学习和成长的过程本身就是回报。它会带来可持续的、有益的多巴胺，让你一直留在游戏之中。

This all sounds great, but I can hear your objection.这一切听起来很棒，但我能听到你的反对意见。

“But Dan, I’ve tried setting a goal like this before, but none of this happens.”“可是 Dan，我以前也试过设定这样的目标，但这些都没有发生。”

Yes.是的。

Your goal needs gravity. It needs to be so strong that everything else in your life seems stupid and pointless.你的目标需要具备足够的分量。它必须强大到让你生活中的其他一切都显得愚蠢且毫无意义。

That’s difficult to do, and I’ll be writing another letter about the intricacies of how to become obsessed with a life-changing goal, but for now let’s learn how to properly set a delusional goal.这很难做到，我会再写一封信，详细探讨如何对一个足以改变人生的目标产生痴迷，但现在我们先来学习如何正确地设定一个妄想型目标。

## How to enter the same reality of ultra successful people如何进入超级成功人士所处的同一现实

> Every great idea starts out as blasphemy. – Anthony De Mello每一个伟大的想法最初都被视为亵渎。 ——安东尼·德梅洛

Steve Jobs would walk into a room, state an impossible deadline or an impossible product as if it were already decided.史蒂夫·乔布斯会走进房间，把一个不可能的最后期限或一个不可能的产品说得好像已经板上钉钉。

“We’ll ship it by January, and it will do this” with such total conviction that the room of engineers who knew it was impossible would leave believing it, and then build toward it like it was simply the plan.“我们一月前发货，它会做到这些。”他说得如此笃定，以至于满屋子明知不可能实现的工程师离开时都信了，然后就像这只是既定计划一样朝着目标去打造。

This was Job’s Reality Distortion Field.这就是乔布斯的现实扭曲力场。

Most people dismiss it as charisma, or manipulation, or him being an asshole, when it was simply him re-aiming the room’s attention.大多数人对此不以为意，认为那不过是魅力、操纵，或者他这人是个混蛋，而实际上他只是在重新引导全场的注意力。

Jobs refused to be pulled by the stimuli of “this timeline can’t possibly work,” and overrode it with a future target. Every brain in the room started selecting for a path instead of selecting for reasons it won’t work.乔布斯拒绝被“这个时间表根本不可能实现”这种刺激牵着走，而是用一个未来目标将其覆盖。房间里的每个人都开始选择一条可行路径，而不是选择它行不通的理由。

And that’s the thing.这就是关键所在。

Your goal is not a destination you hit. That’s one of the greatest self-help-esque interpretations of a goal there is. Instead, a goal is a filter that decides what your brain sees as important and what it ignores. A goal is a point of view. Whether you actually hit the goal or not is irrelevant because you will at least land near the goal, making progress you never thought possible before.你的目标并不是你要抵达的终点。那是对目标最像自助励志式的解读之一。相反，目标是一个过滤器，它决定你的大脑认为什么重要、忽略什么。目标是一种视角。你是否真的达成目标其实无关紧要，因为你至少会落在目标附近，取得你以前从未想过的进步。

To properly set a delusional goal and create your own reality distortion field, here’s what needs to happen.要正确设定一个妄想般的目标并创造你自己的现实扭曲力场，需要做到以下几点。

Pull out a notebook, write these down:拿出一个笔记本，把这些写下来：

**Step 1) Install the point of view.第 1 步）植入这个观点。**

First, think of a goal so big you think it’s impossible.首先，想一个你认为大到不可能实现的目标。

Then, set a timeline so short you think it’s impossible.然后，设定一个短到你觉得不可能完成的时间期限。

If you don’t know what goal to set, or even what to do with your life, it helps to think of the most painful problem in your life right now that makes other problems easier to solve.如果你不知道该设定什么目标，甚至不知道该拿自己的人生怎么办，不妨想一想，眼下生活中哪个最让你痛苦的问题，一旦解决，就能让其他问题更容易解决。

For most people, that’s usually their fitness or finances.对大多数人来说，这通常是他们的健康或财务状况。

Getting in the gym and dropping 20 pounds can do wonders for your confidence and energy, making it easier to start turning your life around.走进健身房，减掉 20 磅，能极大提升你的自信和精力，让你更容易开始扭转人生。

Making more money relieves stress, opens your mind, and lets you invest in other areas of your life.赚更多的钱能缓解压力、开阔你的思维，并让你能够在生活的其他方面进行投入。

The specific “how” for achieving the goal doesn’t need to happen yet. Simply setting the goal lets your mind identify and learn the how.实现目标的具体“方法”还不必现在就出现。仅仅设定目标，就能让你的大脑识别并学习这个方法。

Next, you need to ask yourself one question:接下来，你需要问自己一个问题：

Is the goal and timeline you just set impossible for who you are now? In other words, can your current approach get you there?你刚刚设定的目标和时间线，对现在的你来说是否不可能实现？换句话说，你目前的方法能让你达到那里吗？

If yes, the goal is linear, not impossible. Reject it and raise it further.如果答案是肯定的，那么这个目标是线性的，并非不可能。拒绝它，并把目标再提高一些。

The goal must sit beyond your existing skills, habits, and assumptions of what’s possible because that’s exactly what creates a lens that shows you a new reality instead of a to-do list.目标必须超越你现有的技能、习惯以及对可能性的假设，因为正是这一点创造了一个透镜，让你看到新的现实，而不是一份待办清单。

Last, the goal must be singular and self-chosen.最后，目标必须是单一的，并且是自主选择的。

You cannot have competing goals. That splits focus and doesn’t lead to a point of view at all.你不能同时有彼此冲突的目标。那会分散你的注意力，根本不会让你形成任何观点。

And it cannot be inherited from your parents, your culture, your industry, or your algorithm.而且，它不能从你的父母、你的文化、你的行业或你的算法那里继承而来。

The finished version of setting this goal is when you can say “I do this” with total conviction and you can instantly see that most of what you’re currently doing has nothing to do with it.设定这个目标的最终形态是：你能以十足的信念说出“我做这件事”，并且能立刻看出你目前所做的大部分事情都与它毫无关系。

Welcome to the unknown. The most meaningful place to be.欢迎来到未知。这是最有意义的所在。

**2) Set standards for what you don’t do, and stick to them.2）为你不做的事情设定标准，并坚持下去。**

With a properly set delusional goal, almost nothing will work to achieve it.有了一个设定得当的妄想目标，几乎没有任何办法能实现它。

So, you must now audit your life. Every habit, project, client, relationship, routine, and obligation. Do any of them help you reach the goal? The answer is almost certainly no.所以，你现在必须审视你的生活。每一个习惯、项目、客户、关系、日常安排和义务。它们中有任何一项能帮助你达成目标吗？答案几乎肯定是否定的。

This is the hardest part.这是最困难的部分。

You create a standard that you refuse to lower.你制定了一个你拒绝降低的标准。

These are things you no longer do, period.这些事情你再也不会做了，到此为止。

You don’t “almost” stop partying on the weekends or undercharging for your freelance services, you completely eliminate them.你不会“几乎”停止周末聚会，也不会“几乎”继续对自己的自由职业服务收费过低，而是彻底杜绝它们。

Of course, this is easier said than done. People struggle with this because of sunk cost bias, fear, and a lack of accountability. The mind is a self-deception machine and humans love lying to themselves.当然，这说起来容易做起来难。人们之所以难以做到，是因为沉没成本偏差、恐惧和缺乏问责。大脑是一台自我欺骗的机器，而人类喜欢对自己撒谎。

This is your chance to practice brutal self-honesty and rip multiple band-aids off.这是你练习残酷的自我诚实、一次性撕掉多个创可贴的机会。

The finished version of raising your standards is that life feels dramatically simpler. You have fewer things, fewer obligations, and fewer justifications that clog your mind and prevent you from action.提高标准后的最终状态是，生活变得极其简单。你拥有更少的物品、更少的义务，以及更少堵塞心智、阻碍行动的借口。

**Step 3) Focus on the highest leverage path available to you.第 3 步）专注于你可用的、杠杆效应最高的路径。**

Your goal creates a frame from which you can perceive new opportunities.你的目标会创造一个框架，让你能从中发现新的机会。

Your standards subtract everything from your life that does not serve the goal, creating space for your attention to move in the right direction.你的标准会从你的生活中剔除一切无助于目标的事物，为你的注意力腾出空间，使其朝正确的方向移动。

Now you must accept that there are very few real paths to achieve the goal.现在你必须接受，真正能实现这个目标的路径非常少。

But you don’t find the path, person, or opportunity fully formed.但路径、人或机会都不会以完全成型的状态出现在你面前。

For something like fitness, the delusional goal (impossible outcome + impossible timeline) is to compete in a physique show in 6 months, or squat 405 for the first time this year.以健身为例，妄想型目标（不可能的结果 + 不可能的时间表）是：6 个月内参加形体比赛，或者今年第一次深蹲 405 磅。

You may need a coach who can get you on the right track fast. Your current training program needs to solely focus on the sets, reps, and exercises that will get you there. You start seeing sleep and food as strict and non-negotiable. All things that you didn’t even think about because your previous goal filtered it out as noise.当你设定这个目标后，注意观察你的注意力会发生什么变化。突然之间，“到场、举铁、重复”这种线性路径不再管用了。你需要结构。你可能需要一位能快速把你带上正轨的教练。你当前的训练计划必须只专注于能让你达到目标的组数、次数和动作。你开始把睡眠和饮食视为严格且不可妥协的事。所有这些都是你以前根本没想过的事情，因为你之前的目标把它们当作噪音过滤掉了。
